# Uaena


Uaena is the one-stop website for all Uaenas (IU fanbase) and the public to discover the work of IU (Albums, Shows). Through the new releases section, they can discover the new songs, albums and shows.


Fans and the public can also buy IU merch at the merch page and the home page will display the newest few merch for them to discover. Finally, fans can also sign up to get updates from IU, allowing them to stay up to date on new releases.

## Features
 
### Existing Features
- Feature 1 - allows fans to get updates from IU after signing up.
- Feature 2 - allows fans to discover new releases by adding new-releases section in home page
- Feature 3 - allows fans to listen to the song snippets by having embedded content in the album information
- Feature 3 cont - fans can then choose to listen to the album at the respective websites by clicking on the buttons
-Feature 4 - fans can add a quantity amount for the merch info section instead of having to add one by one. (add 10 quantity at once instead of pressing 10 times)
Feature 5 - cart page for fans to edit their cart however they like, allowing them to increase, decrease or remove items from their cart.
