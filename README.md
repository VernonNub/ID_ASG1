# Uaena


Uaena is the one-stop website for all Uaenas (IU fanbase) and the public to discover the work of IU (Albums, Shows). Through the new releases section, they can discover the new songs, albums and shows.


Fans and the public can also buy IU merch at the merch page and the home page will display the newest few merch for them to discover. Finally, fans can also sign up to get updates from IU, allowing them to stay up to date on new releases.


## Design Process
My design process is simple, to solve user stories based on what i think can most effectively solve it.

- As IU fans, they want to be able to discover and buy her merch, so that they can display their love for IU. 
  - Therefore, our quick and simple merch shop which is also intuitive allows them to purchase their merch from IU officially
- As IU fans, they want to keep up to date with new IU releases so that they can continue to enjoy her music and merchs. 
  - Therefore, we provide a way for them to sign up to receive updates from IU herself. Furthermore, out page has a new release section for fans to discover new release quickly!
- As a general public, they want to discover IU, what her albums are and what are these albums about. 
  - Therefore, we have customised pages for each album giving a brief description of them. Furthermore, these public can use the embedded video to play a short snippet of the songs which they can then click on the link to navigate to the appropriate streaming platforms.

## Features
 
### Existing Features
- Feature 1 - allows fans to get updates from IU after signing up.
- Feature 2 - allows fans to discover new releases by adding new-releases section in home page
- Feature 3 - allows fans to listen to the song snippets by having embedded content in the album information
- Feature 3 cont - fans can then choose to listen to the album at the respective websites by clicking on the buttons
-Feature 4 - fans can add a quantity amount for the merch info section instead of having to add one by one. (add 10 quantity at once instead of pressing 10 times)
Feature 5 - cart page for fans to edit their cart however they like, allowing them to increase, decrease or remove items from their cart.

### Features Left to Implement

- Filter to merch page

## Testing


For any scenarios that have not been automated, test the user stories manually and provide as much detail as is relevant. A particularly useful form for describing your testing process is via scenarios, such as:

Most of the website is automated with javascript and only areas not automated would be the merch section and sign up for newsletter section.

1. Sign Up form:
   1. Go to home page
   2. Try to submit improper email (with no @) and error appears
   3. Submit with proper email and success message appears
2. Buying item form
   1. Go to merch page
   2. Click on merch icons
   3. Directs to appropriate pages with no issues
   4. increase quantity with no issues
   5. decrease quantity with no issues
   6. when quantity is 1, decrease button disables
   7. add to cart and a success message appears
3. Checking out
   1. Click on cart icon
   2. Increase or decrease quantity of each item with no issues
   3. Check that prices change as quantity changes
   4. When quantity is 0, the item disappears
   5. When checking out with all valid inputs, a success message appears


Most css size used in this website's scale of viewport so the website looks rather similar across all platforms. However, some areas like the merch shop and the home page have media queries for a slightly different layout so that the content is easier to see.
